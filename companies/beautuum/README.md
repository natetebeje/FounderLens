## What is Beautuum?

A DTC skincare brand built on three pillars:
1. **Transparent by Design** -- every ingredient explained in plain English
2. **Beautifully Simple** -- Core 4 products that replace full routines
3. **Inclusively Formulated** -- tested across diverse skin tones

**Brand Promise:** *"Skin science, honestly told."*

## Core 4 Product Line

| Product | Function |
|---|---|
| Clarifying Oil Cleanser | Gentle dual-phase, SLS-free |
| Multi-Active Barrier Serum | Niacinamide + peptides + hydration |
| All-Skin Moisturizer | Non-comedogenic, all tones |
| Tinted Mineral SPF 40 | No white cast, inclusive shade range |
